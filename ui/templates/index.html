300GB Multi-Year Alignment, Autobiographical SFT LoRA & Omniscient Era

October 2026

Ingestion of 288 daily vlogs across 2023–2026. Extraction of 1,847 curated SFT Q&A pairs fine-tuned directly into custom GGUF model (sergiu-time-capsule). Omniscient Longitudinal Mode (all_eras) introduced for multi-year panoramic synthesis.
